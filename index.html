Transparency

Permissions explained

We ask for exactly what we need, nothing more.

📱 Phone State
Detect when an incoming call is missed by monitoring call state changes.
📞 Call Log
Required by Android 9+ to get the caller's phone number.
💬 Send SMS
Send your auto-reply text message to the caller's number.
👥 Contacts
Show contact names in the exclusion list. No data is stored or transmitted.
🔔 Notifications
Show a persistent notification indicating the service is active.

These permissions are used exclusively for the app's core function. No data from these permissions is stored, collected, or transmitted outside your device.
